Witches’ Brew Stew

Descirption

Witches’ Brew Stew is a hearty Halloween dinner packed with spicy sausage, ground beef, potatoes, and colorful vegetables in a rich tomato broth. Mushroom “skulls” give this cozy, family-friendly stew a delightfully spooky finish.

Ingredeint

  • 1 pound hot sausage
  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 3 stalks celery, chopped
  • 4 large carrots, sliced
  • 4 large carrots, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 (1-ounce) packet onion soup mix
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 quart beef broth
  • 1 3/4 cups water
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 16 ounces frozen hash brown potatoes
  • 10 ounces frozen cut green beans
  • 1 pound whole button mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Heat a Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add sausage and break it up with a wooden spoon. Add ground beef and cook, with sausage, breaking it up with a wooden spoon or potato masher, until browned and crumbled.
  2. Turn heat to high. Add onions and celery and cook until onion begins to soften, about 1 minute. Stir in carrots and cook for 2 minutes. Add garlic, soup mix, salt, and pepper and cook until fragrant, about 2 minutes.
  3. Stir in broth, tomatoes, water, and bay leaf and bring mixture to a boil, stirring occasionally. Add potatoes and green beans and cook until mixture returns to a boil. Reduce heat slightly and simmer, covered until vegetables are tender.
  4. Meanwhile, cut mushrooms in half vertically. Place mushrooms, flat side down on a work surface and use a straw to poke 2 holes for skeleton “eyes.” Make a third hole for a nose or use a paring knife to make 2 small slits for a nose.
  5. Add mushrooms to the pot and cook until just tender, about 10 minutes longer. Season to taste with salt and pepper if desired and serve with fresh chopped parsley.
